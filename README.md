# PrEP Reach in the Bronx

## Our Project Approach

We are applying **system dynamics group model building (SD GMB)** to support community-engaged, mixed-methods implementation research to inform HIV prevention strategies for people who are at risk of HIV in the Bronx.

Our project brings together community members, researchers, healthcare providers, and other key partners to better understand the complex factors that influence **PrEP reach in the Bronx** and to identify strategies that can improve PrEP engagement.

---

## Project Aims

### AIM 1
**Build and validate a system dynamics model of key factors impacting PrEP reach in the Bronx.**

The first aim focuses on understanding the system of factors that influence PrEP reach and translating these relationships into a system dynamics model.

### AIM 2
**Use the resultant system dynamics model to identify and evaluate high-impact PrEP reach strategies in the Bronx.**

The model will be used to explore potential intervention strategies and examine how different approaches may influence PrEP reach over time.

### AIM 3
**Pilot implementation of the highest-potential PrEP engagement strategies identified in AIM 2.**

The strategies identified through the system dynamics modeling process will inform the development and pilot implementation of a community-informed PrEP engagement program in the Bronx.

---

## Where We Are Now: R33 Phase

### From Modeling to Implementation

Our project is currently in the **R33 phase**.

During the initial **R21 phase**, our work focused on understanding the factors influencing PrEP reach in the Bronx, engaging our community taskforce through system dynamics group model building, and developing and evaluating the system dynamics model.

The **R33 phase** moves the project from model development and strategy evaluation toward **pilot implementation**.

During this phase, we aim to use what we have learned from the system dynamics model and our community-engaged research process to **develop and pilot a program designed to increase PrEP reach in the Bronx**.

---

## Project Timeline

This implementation science project is funded by the **National Institute of Mental Health (NIMH)** for up to five years (**2024–2029**).

### R21 Phase | Years 1–2
**Exploratory/Developmental Grant – Phase I**

Focus:
- Community engagement and group model building
- Understanding factors influencing PrEP reach
- Development and validation of the system dynamics model
- Identification and evaluation of potential PrEP reach strategies

### R33 Phase | Years 3–5
**Exploratory/Developmental Grant – Phase II**

Focus:
- Translation of modeling findings into implementation strategies
- Development of a community-informed pilot program
- Pilot implementation of high-potential PrEP engagement strategies
- Evaluation of the pilot implementation

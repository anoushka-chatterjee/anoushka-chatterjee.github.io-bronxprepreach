Research Timeline

Follow the journey from taskforce engagement and group model building to model-informed intervention planning.

Project progression

How the work develops

This overview highlights major research stages, not a finalized date-by-date record of every session. Individual meeting summaries will be added as approved.

2024

Project launch and taskforce engagement

Establish partnerships, introduce the research goals, and prepare for participatory group model building.

2024–2025

Problem conceptualization

Use group model building sessions to map factors influencing PrEP awareness, access, initiation, and sustained engagement.

2025–2026

Model formulation and refinement

Translate taskforce insights into a system dynamics model; review assumptions, relationships, and model behavior.

2026

Scenario exploration and prioritization

Explore potential intervention approaches and discuss which strategies warrant further development.

2026–2029 · planned

R33 pilot phase

Use findings from modeling and community prioritization to inform development and pilot testing of implementation strategies.

The connection

Why each phase matters

Community discussions shape the model structure; model refinement supports meaningful simulations; simulations help identify and prioritize potential interventions. The timeline makes these connections visible.

Why this work matters

Project overview

We are applying system dynamics group model building (SD GMB) to support community-engaged, mixed-methods implementation research to inform HIV prevention strategies for people at risk of HIV in the Bronx.

Our project brings together community members, researchers, healthcare providers, and other key partners to better understand the complex factors influencing PrEP reach and identify strategies that can improve engagement.

R21 / R33

Project timeline

2024–2026 · YEARS 1–2

R21: Exploratory / Developmental

Community engagement and group model building; understanding factors influencing PrEP reach; model development and validation; identification and evaluation of potential strategies.

2026–2029 · YEARS 3–5

R33: Pilot Implementation

Translate modeling findings into implementation strategies; develop a community-informed pilot program; pilot high-potential strategies; evaluate implementation.

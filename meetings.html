Meetings & Resources

See how group model building helps turn community knowledge into a model that can inform intervention design.

Why the sessions matter

Building the model together

Group model building (GMB) is central to the research process. Taskforce members help identify barriers to PrEP reach, describe relationships among factors, challenge assumptions, and consider which strategies may be feasible and impactful.

The meeting summaries below are organized by research stage. Session-by-session summaries and approved materials can be added as they are reviewed for public sharing.

From discussion to decisions

How meeting insights shape the research

PHASE 1

Understand the problem

Discuss experiences and system-level barriers; identify factors and feedback relationships relevant to PrEP engagement.

PHASE 2

Develop and validate the model

Review model structure and assumptions, identify gaps, and refine the representation of the local system.

PHASE 3

Explore strategies

Use the model and taskforce discussion to consider intervention scenarios and implementation priorities.

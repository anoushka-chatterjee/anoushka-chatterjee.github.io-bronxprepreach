Our Research

Research priorities

Our three research aims

AIM 1

Build & Validate

Build and validate a system dynamics model of key factors impacting PrEP reach in the Bronx.

AIM 2

Identify & Evaluate

Use the model to identify and evaluate high-impact strategies to improve PrEP reach.

AIM 3

Pilot Implementation

Pilot implementation of the highest-potential PrEP engagement strategies identified in Aim 2.

Our methods

System dynamics group model building

System dynamics group model building is a participatory approach for exploring how multiple, interconnected factors shape outcomes over time. Our project uses this approach to support community-engaged implementation research.

Community insights inform the development of a system dynamics model, which can be used to examine how different strategies may influence PrEP reach in the Bronx.

Research pathway

From evidence to action

Our work connects community knowledge, model development, strategy evaluation, and pilot implementation to inform HIV prevention efforts.

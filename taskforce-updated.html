Meet our members

Our taskforce brings together expertise in community health, clinical care, HIV prevention, research, public service, and data science. Select “Read biography” to learn more about individual members.

AA

Angela Aifah, PhD, AM

Institute for Excellence in Health Equity and Department of Population Health, NYU Grossman School of Medicine

Read biography

Angela Aifah is an Assistant in Global Health within the Institute for Excellence in Health Equity and the Department of Population Health at NYU Grossman School of Medicine. A social work-trained researcher, her work focuses on evidence-informed implementation science to address the dual burden of communicable and noncommunicable diseases in low-resource, high-need contexts, with an emphasis on community-engaged and partnership-driven approaches to integrating health services for comorbid conditions.

GA

Gustavo Adolfo Morales Correa

Latino Commission on AIDS

Read biography

Gustavo Morales is a sexual health professional and public speaker, currently serving New York City and Puerto Rico as Lead Capacity Building Specialist for Hands United. He previously directed the Latino Commission on AIDS’ Prevention and Access to Care Department, leading HIV testing initiatives and a major mpox vaccination campaign. Over his 24-year career, he has advanced HIV prevention and treatment for the Latinx LGBT community through training, program development, and collaborations with public health leaders. Born in Toa Baja, Puerto Rico, Gustavo began HIV advocacy at 17 and continues to champion equitable sexual health strategies.

Raisha Hernandez

Office of Community and Population Health, Montefiore Medical Center

Read biography

With over 10 years of experience in public health and education, Raisha Hernandez is committed to advancing community welfare and improving health literacy. Her work centers on promoting health equity while empowering communities through advocacy, education, and engagement. She strives to create healthier, more resilient communities.

BK

Benjamin Katz

Institute for Implementation in Population Health, City University of New York

Read biography

Benjamin Katz is the Senior Project Manager of the Dashboard Team at the CUNY ISPH. His work centers on the design and development of web-based data visualization platforms to track and disseminate key population-level health outcomes. With a focus on accessibility and usability, the ISPH Dashboard Team strives to transform and visualize complex data into actionable information that enhances learning and informs policy and programmatic decision-making at all levels of the public health landscape.

David W. Lounsbury

Department of Epidemiology & Population Health, Albert Einstein College of Medicine

Read biography

David Lounsbury is an Associate Professor of Epidemiology and Population Health and Associate Director of Patient-Centered Outcomes Research Training at the Albert Einstein College of Medicine. As a community psychologist and systems scientist, he designs and conducts research that strives to strengthen community partnerships to improve the way health care works in the Bronx and beyond.

JM

Jose Mazariego

CUNY Graduate School of Public Health and Health Policy

Read biography

Jose Mazariego is a doctoral student in Epidemiology at the CUNY Graduate School of Public Health and Health Policy (CUNY SPH). He is affiliated with the Center for Immigrant, Refugee, and Global Health, where his research focuses on health disparities among immigrant and refugee communities in New York City and State. He also collaborates with researchers studying migrant health in large cities. Previously, at the Center for Systems and Community Design at CUNY SPH, he worked with Drs. Nasim Sabounchi and David Lounsbury on system dynamics modeling projects, including HPV vaccine uptake among adolescents in New York State and substance use, homelessness, and psychological distress among people living with HIV.

Jonathan Ross

Division of General Internal Medicine, Albert Einstein College of Medicine; Montefiore Health System

Read biography

Dr. Ross completed his residency training at Montefiore in the Primary Care/Social Internal Medicine program and received a Master’s in Clinical Research Methods from Albert Einstein College of Medicine. As a board-certified internist and AAHIVM HIV Specialist, he provides HIV/AIDS treatment and prevention as well as general medical care at Montefiore’s Wakefield Ambulatory Care Center. LS

Liz Spurrell-Huss, MSW, MPH

Office of Community and Population Health, Montefiore Medical Center

Read biography

Liz Spurrell-Huss is Director of Community Health Programs in Montefiore Health System’s Office of Community & Population Health. She leads patient health education and community health programming and co-leads the system’s social needs screening and referral initiative, developing clinical-community partnerships to address social determinants of health. She is also a principal educator at the Albert Einstein College of Medicine, facilitating first-year medical students’ introduction to strategic community engagement. Previously, she worked as a clinical social worker for HIV-positive youth and survivors of trauma and led the Bronx-wide HIV prevention coalition Connect2Protect. She serves on the Steering Committee of the #Not62 Campaign for Healthier Bronx.

CM

Carolina Miranda, MD

Montefiore CHP Practice, Family Medicine, Primary Care

Read biography

Carolina Miranda is a family medicine physician at Montefiore Medical Center and an Assistant Professor of Family and Social Medicine at the Albert Einstein College of Medicine. She provides comprehensive primary care to immigrant families at the Montefiore Bronx Health Collective, with a clinical focus on individuals seeking asylum, people experiencing homelessness, and members of the LGBTQIA+ community. She also teaches medical students clinical and communication skills.

EN

Eric Nieves

PrEP Patient Navigator, Montefiore Medical Group HIV Prevention Program

Read biography

Eric Nieves is the Program Assistant for the Montefiore Medical Group HIV Prevention Program. The program helps connect Bronx residents at high risk of HIV with PrEP services, including assistance with insurance, adherence, and appointment scheduling. It also connects individuals newly diagnosed with HIV to primary care to initiate treatment.

RR

Raffy Rios

Montefiore Children’s Hospital

Read biography

Raffy is a community health liaison with over a decade of experience advocating for survivors of intimate partner violence, sexual violence, and hate violence. They are passionate about education and prevention for communities most impacted by HIV/AIDS and increasing access to resources in the Bronx and surrounding neighborhoods. Raffy is also co-founder of NYC Gaymers, a nonprofit that uses gaming to connect communities with information, education, and safer spaces.
